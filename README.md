### First is **Rising shield hero**

Series Overview

-The Rising of the Shield Hero follows Naofumi Iwatani, one of four men summoned from modern-day Japan to the kingdom of Melromarc as legendary heroes tasked with stopping the destructive Waves of Catastrophe. Unlike the others, Naofumi receives the Shield, making him the weakest in offense and causing others to look down on him.

After being falsely accused and betrayed by Malty Melromarc, Naofumi is shunned by society and loses trust in others. To survive and grow stronger, he purchases a dying demi-human slave named Raphtalia, who becomes his loyal companion. Together, they overcome challenges, build their strength, and fight to protect the kingdom from the approaching Waves while Naofumi works to clear his name and prove his worth as the Shield Hero.
**Main Character**

Who is **NAOFUMI IWATANI**?

- Origin: A 20-year-old Japanese college sophomore and otaku who gets summoned into a fantasy parallel world after reading a book about the Four Cardinal Heroes.
- Role: Wielder of the Legendary Shield, one of the Four Cardinal Heroes tasked with fighting apocalyptic events called "Waves".
- Personality Shift: Initially open-minded and hopeful, he becomes deeply cynical, pragmatic, and distrustful after being betrayed, robbed, and falsely accused of a crime by Princess Malty Melromarc.
- Combat Style: Because the Shield lacks direct offensive power, he relies heavily on defense, tactical planning, slave/party contracts, and unlocking curse or craft-based shield variants to protect his companions.
- Key Allies: His core party includes Raphtalia (a raccoon demi-human sword fighter) and Filo (a bird-like Filolial shape-shifter).

### Second is **Black Clover**

Series Overview

-Black Clover is a Japanese fantasy manga and anime series written and illustrated by Yūki Tabata. The story follows Asta and Yuno, two orphans abandoned at a church in Hage Village. In a world where everyone is born with the natural ability to use magic (mana), Asta is a rare exception who possesses zero magical power. Meanwhile, Yuno grows up as a wind-magic prodigy.

Who is **ASTA**?

- Origin: Born without any magical power (mana) in a world where magic is everything, he was left as an infant at a church in Hage Village alongside his foster brother, Yuno.
- Goal: He dreams of becoming the Wizard King—the highest rank of mage in the Clover Kingdom—to end class discrimination and prove that hard work can surpass innate talent.
- Powers & Equipment: Despite having zero magic, he receives a rare five-leaf clover grimoire that houses Anti-Magic, allowing him to nullify and cut through magical attacks using heavy swords (such as the Demon-Slayer and Demon-Dweller). He fights alongside an anti-magic devil named Liebe, using forms like Black Asta and Devil Union.
- Physical Stats: To compensate for his lack of magic, he has built superhuman physical strength, speed, and mastery over Ki sensing through rigorous daily training.
- Affiliation: A former member of the Black Bull Magic Knight squad.

### Third is **Bleach**
Series Overview

 is a Japanese manga and anime series created by Tite Kubo that follows Ichigo Kurosaki, a 15-year-old high school student who can see ghosts.

 Who is **Ichigo**?

- Role: A teenager who accidentally becomes a Substitute Soul Reaper (Shinigami) after meeting the Soul Reaper Rukia Kuchiki.
- Heritage: He is a rare, complex hybrid of Human, Soul Reaper, Hollow, Quincy, and Fullbringer lineage.
- Name Meaning: "Ichigo" translates to strawberry in Japanese, can mean "the one who protects," and represents the numbers 1 and 5 (ichi-go).
- Family: He is the son of Isshin Kurosaki and the late Masaki Kurosaki, older brother to Karin and Yuzu, husband to Orihime Inoue, and father to Kazui Kurosaki.

Powers & Abilities

- Zanpakutō: Wields the blade Zangetsu (Shikai) and its Bankai form, Tensa Zangetsu.
- Signature Move: Getsuga Tenshō, a powerful wave of spiritual energy fired from his sword.
- Hybrid Enhancements: Combines Soul Reaper swordsmanship with Hollowfication (masks and Cero blasts), Quincy traits like Blut Vene, and Fullbring energy attacks
